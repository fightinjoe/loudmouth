Return JSON only, with exactly one conversation object for every checklist entry, in the same order, preserving each title exactly, including duplicate titles. Use exactly this structure and no other keys:

AUTHOR EACH TOPIC IN THIS ORDER:
1. Essential phrases: choose what the learner needs to say to accomplish this topic, independently of any example story. Give 1 to 8 short, complete, reusable phrases spoken by "you", never by the partner. Lead with an immediately usable way to initiate this topic or state the learner's purpose. Cover distinct likely intents, options, and useful follow-ups, not near-synonyms or one story's transitions. Not every essential must be an opener. Let real complexity determine the count; do not fill a quota.
2. Useful words: choose 0 to 10 English dictionary words or short fixed compounds directly from this situation's needs, not by extracting the example dialogue. Use singular nouns and base verbs; include useful verbs, adjectives, and adverbs where appropriate, not only nouns. No full sentences, padding, or encyclopedic lists. A word may recur under different topics when useful for each. Use an empty array if there are genuinely no useful standalone words; never omit vocab.
3. Example conversation: illustrate a useful subset in a short worked exchange, including likely partner replies. Do not make the essentials or word list depend on what happens to occur here. Sharing wording naturally is fine; do not force every item into the dialogue or invent a quota of items outside it.

STYLE AND ABILITY:

Do not invent personal details, dietary exceptions, symptoms, allergies, or skill levels as known facts. - conversational: the learner handles everyday small talk. Focus on this situation's phrases and unexpected but likely partner replies.

- Follow the requested topic. Generic conversation repair should not displace situational needs, but clarification, repetition, or asking for help belongs when the checklist actually requests it.

When useful, place an alternative immediately after that speaker's entry. Each entry has only speaker and text; do not label alternatives. No nested branches or multi-turn trees.

All strings must be nonblank. Output only the JSON object, without prose or code fences.
