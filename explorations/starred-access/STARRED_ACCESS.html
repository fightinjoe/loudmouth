Catchphrase / finalized design reference

Starred, within reach.

Browse a phrasebook’s starred cards from a fixed leftmost tab. The tab row reads as one surface at rest; its divider and shadow reveal the pinned layer only while topics scroll beneath it.

Design contract

Layout and state

Adopted in the web app; native adoption remains separate. This reference specifies starred-card browsing inside a saved phrasebook, not a Review session. Keep existing card actions and phrasebook page order. Implement the behavior below using the target framework’s layout, scrolling, and accessibility primitives; the HTML structure is not an implementation requirement.

  • Structure: phrasebook title, one tab row, then an independently scrolling card list. Starred is the fixed leftmost tab, outside the horizontal topic scroller but part of the same navigation. Keep the existing Translations/conversation pages, optional Chunks, and Vocab order. The title and tabs do not scroll vertically with the cards.
  • Pinned tab: show a solid blue star and the exact count of unique starred memberships; no visible “Starred” label. The star stays blue regardless of the active page. Use the active-tab underline to indicate selection. The tab has an opaque white surface above the scrolling topics; its intrinsic width must grow with the count.
  • Scroll edge: the full-height divider and trailing shadow are always part of the design, never preferences. Show both only when the topic strip’s horizontal offset is greater than zero. At zero or negative elastic overscroll, hide both. This is based on actual scrolling, not the selected page. Shadow and divider do not consume layout space or intercept input.
No starsNo Starred tab, reserved width, divider, or shadow.
First starReveal the tab without opening it or changing the selected topic. Update every occurrence of that membership.
Open StarredShow the current phrasebook’s starred collection. Keep the topic strip’s current horizontal position and remember the previously active page.
Unstar a cardRemove its collection row and update the count; retain the saved card and membership. Keep the current page unless the collection becomes empty.
Remove last starRemove the tab immediately. If Starred was active, return to the previous page and restore focus to its tab; otherwise keep the active page.

Card presentation: English appears above the target, with readings wherever supplied. Chinese uses pinyin above characters; Japanese follows the phrasebook’s script/furigana or romaji setting. Learner speech is right-aligned blue; partner speech is left-aligned neutral, with text left-aligned on both. Occurrence alternative: true produces “or” on the same speaker side. Starred is a neutral, full-width list, not reconstructed dialogue. Words retain definitions; Chunks show their full original-script source snapshot with the saved span highlighted. Missing optional content adds no empty rows.

Geometry and animation

Center within the viewport. Stop at the edges.

  1. Interior topics: target the full phrasebook tab-row viewport’s midpoint, including the pinned tab’s occupied area. In one coordinate system, compute targetScroll = currentScroll + selectedTabMidpoint − viewportMidpoint, then clamp to [0, max(0, contentWidth − scrollableWidth)]. Equivalently, subtract half the current pinned width from the scrollable area’s midpoint. For a 390-unit viewport and a 60-unit pinned tab, the target is x = 195, not x = 225.
  2. First and last topics: first targets scroll zero, aligned left beside Starred; last targets the maximum scroll offset, aligned right. A single scrolling tab aligns left. These rules also apply to oversized titles. Do not add leading/trailing spacers to force centering.
  3. Dynamic geometry: measure actual viewport, tab, content, and pinned widths. Do not hard-code count width. Recompute after resize, font/layout changes, or membership changes. Preserve each page’s vertical scroll position independently.
  4. One coordinated update: apply pinned width and scroll compensation together before paint/layout presentation. Track the selected tab’s visual position, not a stale scroll target. During width changes, a centered interior topic stays at the viewport center; an aligned first topic follows the strip’s left boundary; an aligned last topic stays at its right boundary. Preserve a manually scrolled offset rather than forcing recentering.
  5. Interruption: a new selection starts from the current visible position. Direct pointer/touch scrolling or wheel input takes over. Selecting pinned Starred stops selection-driven strip motion without moving the strip. Avoid separate completion-time corrections that cause a jump.
Tab selection280 ms cubic ease-out: 1 − (1 − progress)³. Same behavior for pointer and keyboard selection.
Starred entrance360 ms cubic ease-out, expanding width and fading in at the left edge. Runs when the first star is added or a phrasebook with stars is initially presented. Ordinary count changes and page selection do not replay it.
Divider + shadowFade together over 180 ms ease-out in both directions as the strip leaves or returns to zero.
Reduced motionPosition immediately and skip fades/entrance motion. Enabling reduced motion during animation must settle to valid geometry without partial widths.

Visual reference values: star/count gap 6 px; pinned padding 12 px per side; topic padding 16 px per side; underline 3 px. Divider: 1 px, full tab height, #ced7e3. Shadow: 10 px wide, fading horizontally from rgba(15,24,42,.09) to transparent; additionally fade vertically from transparent at the top to full strength at 12 px, avoiding a hard top edge. Star blue #0877c9; underline #1e9aff. Cards have 16 px corners and 8 px gaps. The specimen’s 390 × 700 px frame is a demonstration size, not an app constraint. Adapt logical units and fonts to the platform while preserving these relationships and accessible text sizing.

Platform handoff

Preserve behavior, not DOM structure.

  • Data: stars belong to the current phrasebook’s memberships, not global cards. Count and list each starred membership once. For a repeated Phrase, use its first displayed occurrence and that occurrence’s translation. Use phrasebook display order, not star time or shuffled Review order. Use stable group/occurrence/card identities, not titles. Unstarring never deletes cards or source evidence. Follow the card schema; no schema migration is required.
  • Accessibility: expose a selectable tab named “Starred, N cards,” selected state, and its associated content. Card stars expose their pressed state. Provide visible keyboard focus, nonwrapping arrows and Home/End navigation, and touch targets of at least 44 × 44 logical units or the platform minimum if larger. Removing a focused collection row moves focus to the next available star, or the preceding star at the end; the last-star rule above takes precedence. Count updates should be announced without moving focus.
  • Integration: retain tab identity across card/count updates so scrolling, focus, and edge fades are not reset. Keep offscreen content out of navigation/accessibility traversal. This HTML deliberately omits audio, details, Review, editing, persistence, and page-swipe gestures; preserve their existing platform contracts rather than treating those omissions as design decisions.

Base contracts: product brief, current app UX, and pane behavior. This document supplies the Starred access design, now reflected in the current web UX document. Web integration points are page/tab rendering, active-page and membership state, and joined collection selection. Native implementations should map the same contract to native components.

Verification boundary: desktop Chromium checks cover the remaining controls, all four language modes, narrow-screen wrapping, automatic entrance, edge visibility, alignment, membership transitions, keyboard focus, and reduced motion. For each target platform, also sample geometry during count-width changes and interrupted animations; verify accessible text sizing, reading order, and device gesture arbitration. Physical iPhone Safari, VoiceOver, and native Dynamic Type remain unverified. Fixture language is illustrative, not validated teaching content.
