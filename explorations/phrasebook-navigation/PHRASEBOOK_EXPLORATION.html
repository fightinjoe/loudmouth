Catchphrase / adopted navigation study

Keep the conversations
within reach.

Conversation context tabs are the approved navigation direction adopted into the web app. Interior tabs center while the first and final selections anchor to the strip edges.

Methodology · Behavior & observations

Scroll the tab strip, select a conversation, or swipe phrases left and right; desktop mouse dragging also works. Interior selections center, the first tab stays left, and the final Vocab tab stays right. Full titles remain in accessible names and hover tooltips. Stars update independently of speaker backgrounds; this specimen does not include audio, review, details-pane, or persistence behavior.

Selected direction · Figma styles

Swipeable conversation tabs

White surfaces, cool-gray phrases, blue emphasis, and Figma typography support one conversation page at a time in a single-phone study.

Approved · adopted into web

Less metadata. Continuous movement.

InteractionOne tab and one page per conversation, followed by Vocab. No repeated headings, counts, or Conversation N labels. Tabs occupy 50% of the viewport with no end spacers. First selection aligns its tab’s left edge with the strip; final selection aligns its right edge. Interior selections center, exposing half of a neighbor on each side. A single empty-state Vocab tab anchors left. Clicking or keyboard-selecting animates the strip and card pages for 280 ms with cubic ease-out. A horizontal drag follows the pointer; release beyond 45 px advances one page, otherwise it eases back. Vertical gestures retain native scrolling. Interrupted motion continues from the current position. Resize aligns without animation; reduced motion skips animations. Vertical position survives tab changes; offscreen pages are inert. Keyboard arrows and Home/End work without looping.

Adaptation boundaryThe referenced frame is a grouped list, not the tabbed conversation treatment. The study retains offset speaker sides and 8 px gaps. Cards are borderless: left-side speech is gray-50, right-side speech is blue-100. Consecutive lines and alternatives from the same speaker share a color; swapping sides swaps the color assignment. Stars change only the icon, never the speaker background. Vocab remains neutral gray. Additional space beside the target text protects the star from long phrases. Readings use 11/14 px ruby and 16/18 px romaji; CJK glyphs use available system fallback because the referenced frame defines Latin text. OS status bars, menu/settings actions, group headers/counts, and the floating action bar are outside this study. Manrope and Roboto Condensed load from Google Fonts with display=swap; loading the web fonts requires a network connection, with sans-serif fallback while unavailable. The original Figma star SVG geometry is embedded in this HTML and needs no external assets.
