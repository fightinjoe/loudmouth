Catchphrase / new-phrasebook-pane study · iteration 1

Pick a language.
Then what?

Question. The new-phrasebook action pane currently asks one thing, the target language, through a native select, and hands off to guided creation. The navigation study moved suggested phrasebooks off the landing page and onto this pane. With two things to offer, what should the pane look like: a language choice with suggestions as an optional shortcut, or a language choice followed by two explicit paths?

Boundary. Two competing treatments of the same action pane (action layer), shown over the converged navigation pane. Language and suggestion selection really work. Tapping a primary button, field or suggestion row shows the transition it would fire. Not in scope: the creation flow itself (situation, questions, conversation checklist), the suggestion preview, ability memory per language, the sheet’s open/close animation and swipe-down dismissal. Suggestions for Spanish and Chinese are hypothetical fixtures; only the Japanese set exists in the app today.

Methodology · UX design · Pane Protocol · Navigation-pane study · Decision record

Change the language inside either sheet and the suggestions follow it. Czech has no suggestions, which is the case where that section must disappear cleanly. In approach 1 a tapped suggestion becomes the selected choice and reveals the ability question; in approach 2 a suggestion row opens its preview directly.

1 · Pick and go

One sheet, one button

Content-hugging beige sheet, the current sheet’s size and surface. Languages are a white list with a check mark instead of a native select. Suggestions for the chosen language are dashed chips under it. Choosing a chip turns the button into “View” and reveals the ability question, matching the app’s existing confirmation gate. Otherwise the button continues to guided creation.

Language up top, then choose how to start

Full-height gray sheet, the size the creation and review panes already use. Languages are a row of flag tiles. Status: exploring

StatusExploring. Two treatments awaiting feedback; none selected.
QuestionWith suggested phrasebooks moved into this pane, should it stay a single-purpose language gate with suggestions as an optional shortcut (1), or become the place where the learner chooses between describing a situation and picking a suggestion (2)?
What differs
  • Sheet size and surface. 1 hugs its content on beige, as today. 2 is full-height on gray-100, as creation and review are today.
  • Language control. 1: a white list with a check, one tap. 2: flag tiles in a row, one tap. Both replace the native select; both assume four languages, the app’s current list.
  • Suggestions. 1: selectable chips; a selection changes the primary button to “View …” and reveals ability, so the pane still has one button. 2: rows that open the preview directly; ability would be asked on the preview.
  • Flow. 1 keeps the current sequence: language, then creation mode asks for the situation. 2 asks for the situation here and would enter creation mode at the questions step. That is a change to DESIGN.md’s guided-creation sequence and needs a separate decision.
Data assumptionsLanguages are ["zh","ja","es","cs"] from new-phrasebook-panel.js. Suggestions are pendingSuggestions filtered by language; only Japanese entries exist today, the Spanish and Chinese ones here are hypothetical. Ability values are none, basics, conversational and default to the language’s last-used value or “basics”. The preselected language would be the last-used language rather than a fixed default; the study exposes it as a control.
