# Improve phrase breakdown and extend it to Words

Part of the [backlog](../backlog.md).

- **What:** review the design and teaching content of the [phrase breakdown](../../design.md#phrase-breakdown-web) and improve both, including a breakdown for individual Word cards (for example inflection, related forms, usage notes and example sentences).
- **Why:** owner request, 2026-11-12. Breakdown today analyzes one Phrase occurrence into source-aligned chunks with nested dictionary Words ([`/phrase-breakdown`](../../api/phrase-breakdown.md)); Word cards have no breakdown. Content changes go through the [phrase-breakdown evaluation](../../evals/phrase-breakdown.md), and prompt edits need owner approval first.
- **Done when:** review findings are recorded in the [results ledger](../../evals/results.md), adopted design and content changes ship, and Word cards open a breakdown in the web app.
